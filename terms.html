terms

the short, honest version. using gymgyme means you are okay with these.

what gymgyme is

a free community directory of home training resources and a browser-based personal trainer that reads your movement through your camera, on your device. it is free, with no ads.

not medical advice

gymgyme is not a doctor, a physiotherapist or a certified trainer. the cues and scores help you move with more awareness; they are not a diagnosis, a treatment or a training prescription. warm up, go at your own pace, and stop if anything hurts. if you have a health condition or an injury, talk to a professional before exercising.

your own risk

exercise carries risk. you are responsible for your own body, your space and your equipment. gymgyme is provided as is, without warranties; to the extent the law allows, we are not liable for injuries or damages arising from your training.

accounts

an account is optional. keep your password to yourself; the account is for one person. you can delete your synced workouts or your whole account at any time from the personal trainer page. we may remove accounts that abuse the service.

content in the directory

directory links point to third-party sites we do not control. suggestions are reviewed by a human before appearing, but follow linked instructions at your own judgement.
