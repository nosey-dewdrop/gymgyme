so, how does it count? in plain words 🎀

no chatgpt. no server watching you. just geometry, running on your phone.

it does not "understand" you the way a chatbot pretends to. it measures you. a squat is your knee closing to an angle and opening back up. the engine watches that angle the whole way down and the whole way back, and only then does it call it one clean rep.

  1. 1
    your camera finds 33 points on your body. an open pose model already trained by google runs on your device and marks your shoulders, hips, knees, ankles. it returns them in real 3d, so the math stays honest even when you turn toward the lens.
  2. 2
    three points make an angle. hip, knee, ankle draw the bend behind your knee. the engine reads that angle every single frame, smooths out the jitter, and knows the difference between the bottom of your squat and standing tall.
  3. 3
    a full down and up is one rep. it waits for the whole cycle to close before the tick. a little wobble in the middle does not sneak in as two. that is why the count matches what you actually did.
  4. 4
    then it grades the rep out of 100. three honest signals: how deep you really went, how your tempo compares to the move, and how steady the angle stayed. strict, never mean.
