your camera finds you

a small open pose model spots 33 points of your body, on your device - like motion capture, minus the ping-pong ball suit.

our engine does the math

written in c++, compiled to webassembly, running inside your browser at camera speed. it learns YOUR body first, then locks onto you.

every full rep gets a tick you can hear. half reps get called out, kindly. sets and rests run themselves.

depth, tempo and control, scored 0-100. honest, never mean. your form gets corrected in 3d while you move.
