how you use it

browse the moves, then turn them into your own workout.

this is the whole loop. pick what you like, save it, arrange it into a plan, then stand in front of the camera and do it.

01 browse

find your moves

386 acts, zero equipment. pilates, ballet, yoga, bodyweight. search or filter by muscle.

02 save

keep the ones you like

tap to add a move to your moves. build a shortlist that is actually yours.

03 build

make a workout

drop saved moves onto the days you train. reps for counted moves, seconds for holds.

04 do it

open the camera

run your workout with the trainer watching. it counts, scores, and corrects the form.

everything reversible, same session. add and remove, schedule and unschedule.

how does it count?

no chatgpt, no server. just geometry running on your phone.

1

it reads you

an open pose model marks 33 points of your body every frame, in 3d, on your own screen.

2

it measures the angle

a squat is your knee angle closing and opening. it watches that curve, not a picture of you.

3

it counts the rep

only a full, clean cycle counts. half-hearted wobbles never sneak a second rep in.

4

it forgets the frame

the math is done, the frame is gone. nothing stored, nothing sent, nothing kept.

hand-written in c++, compiled to keep pace with your camera. 92 native tests. no api bill, which is exactly why it stays free.

so where does the video go?

nowhere.

there is no upload button, because there is no upload code. the camera feed lives for a blink in your browser and is gone. it cannot leak, because it never exists anywhere but here.
