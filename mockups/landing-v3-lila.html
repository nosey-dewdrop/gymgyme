How it counts

No ChatGPT, no server watching you. Just geometry running on your phone.

Every move is a spec: which joint drives it, what angle counts as the bottom, what counts as lockout. The math runs frame by frame, right where you stand.

1

It reads your body.

An open pose model returns 33 points of you per frame, in 3D, on your own GPU.

2

It measures the angle.

A squat is your knee angle closing and opening. The engine watches that curve, not a picture of you.

3

It counts the rep.

Only a full, clean cycle counts, with hysteresis, so half-hearted wobbles never double up.

4

It throws the frame away.

The math is done, the frame is gone. Nothing is stored, nothing is sent.

Written by hand in C++, compiled to run at camera speed. 92 native tests. No API bill, which is exactly why it stays free.
