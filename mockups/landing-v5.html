how it counts

no chatgpt, no server squinting at you. just geometry, running on your phone.

your joints make angles, angles make reps. that is the whole trick, and it happens right where you stand.

1

it reads you.

an open pose model marks 33 points of your body every frame, in 3d, on your own screen.

2

it measures the angle.

a squat is your knee angle closing and opening. it watches that curve, not a picture of you.

3

it counts the rep.

only a full, clean cycle counts. half hearted wobbles never sneak a second rep in.

4

it forgets the frame.

the math is done, the frame is gone. nothing stored, nothing sent, nothing kept.

hand written in c++, compiled to keep up with your camera instead of your patience. 92 native tests. no api bill, which is exactly why it stays free.

okay but how, in human words

is it chatgpt?no. no llm, no api call, no server thinking for you. it is trigonometry running at camera speed.
then what sees me?an open, already trained pose model on your own gpu. it returns 33 landmarks a frame and none of them go anywhere.
did you train it on my body?no. each frame does its math in memory and is thrown away. there is no training pipeline to feed you into, even if i wanted one.
so how did you make it?i did not train, i engineered. the pose model came pre trained. on top sits my own engine, tuned move by move on myself.
who does the counting?my own engine, written in c++ and compiled to run in your browser. one math pass per frame, so it keeps pace with you.
and why is it free?because it costs me nothing to run. no cluster, no api, no server bill. so there is nothing to charge you for.
