the pose and all the math run inside your browser. the camera image never leaves this device - there is no upload and nothing is stored.

the coach watches six moves (squat, push-up, lunge, glute bridge, sit-up, press), counts full reps with a tick, calls out half reps that did not count, scores every rep from depth, tempo and control, and corrects your form in 3d - knees out, chest up, hips in line. set a plan and it counts your sets, rests you between them and rings when the workout is done. a full session summary is next.
