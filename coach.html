personal trainer

stand back so your whole body fits the frame, then press start. your device does the seeing and the thinking - no server ever gets the video.

go at your own pace and warm up first. stop if anything hurts. your personal trainer reads your form to help you move better - it is not medical advice, and it does not replace a doctor.

a plan, if you like: in resting leave reps at 0 to just count freely.

the pose and all the math run inside your browser. the camera image never leaves this device - there is no upload. if you are signed in, only your workout numbers (never the video) are saved to your account. your data.

the personal trainer watches six moves (squat, push-up, lunge, glute bridge, sit-up, press), counts full reps with a tick, calls out half reps that did not count, scores every rep from depth, tempo and control, and corrects your form in 3d - knees out, chest up, hips in line. set a plan and it counts your sets, rests you between them and rings when the workout is done, then hands you a small summary: reps, sets, how long, your average and best, how many came with clean form.
