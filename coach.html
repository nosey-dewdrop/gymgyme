the personal trainer watches fourteen moves - squats and lunges, push-ups on toes or knees, bridge, sit-up, press, kickback, bird dog, calf raise, jumping jack, arm raise - counts full reps with a tick, calls out half reps that did not count, scores every rep from depth, tempo and control, and corrects your form in 3d. it learns your body first and locks onto you. set a plan and it counts your sets, rests you between them and rings when the workout is done, then hands you a small summary.
